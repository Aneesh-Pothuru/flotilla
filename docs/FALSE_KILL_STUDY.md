# False-kill study protocol

1. Before outcomes are known, sample at least 20% of predicate-confirmed kills
   with a seeded random selection and publish the seed and sample manifest.
2. Run each sampled thesis's pre-registered full plan without changing data,
   scores, or acceptance thresholds.
3. A kill is correct when the full plan also rejects the registered prediction.
   A full run that validates the prediction is a false kill. Inconclusive or
   invalid full runs are `UNDETERMINED` and stay out of both numerators while
   remaining in the published abstention denominator.
4. Publish the early-kill accuracy, false-kill rate, Wilson 95% intervals,
   abstention rate, full lineage, and all selection exclusions.
5. Do not claim the brief's ≥90%/≤5% targets until interval-aware results from
   a useful sample exist.
