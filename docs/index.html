The portfolio problem

Research teams optimize experiments. Few optimize what deserves an experiment.

A leaderboard cannot tell a research director when to stop, what budget was returned, or whether yesterday’s kill should be challenged. FLOTILLA treats those choices as first-class, reviewable artifacts.

01 · Fair floor

Every thesis gets one honest chance.

The scheduler funds the cheapest registered falsifier for every thesis before any survivor receives follow-up capital.

02 · Executable rules

Models propose. Predicates dispose.

Kill conditions are allowlisted expressions evaluated against recorded scores. Missing evidence becomes UNDETERMINED, never a guess.

03 · Reversible governance

A stop is a decision, not deletion.

Human confirmation is mandatory. REVIVE appends a challenge while preserving the original evidence, confirmer, and lineage.

Operating doctrine

Designed for research leaders, legible to reviewers.

FLOTILLA is not a new experiment tracker or autonomous lab. It is the governance layer that makes allocation decisions explicit, executable, contestable, and reproducible.

Research director

See the whole mandate.

Compare thesis trajectories and actual capital flow before moving reserve toward surviving work.

Thesis owner

Register what would change your mind.

Pair every claim with a cheap falsifier, a deadline, a budget cap, and limitations before dispatch.

Independent reviewer

Audit or challenge the stop.

Inspect scores, data identity, code commit, confirmer, and the exact predicate before confirming, overturning, or reviving.
